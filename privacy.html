Privacy Policy
Effective Date: May 7, 2026  ·  BCPS Field Log Application

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Barton Creek Property Solutions ("BCPS," "we," "us") handles information in connection with the BCPS Field Log application. This is an internal business tool used exclusively by BCPS staff and authorized personnel for daily field reporting and project management.

Information We Collect

The BCPS Field Log app collects and stores the following types of information entered by authorized users:

  • Daily field log entries (project, date, crew, work performed, materials, incidents)
  • Punch list items and completion status
  • Task assignments and project data
  • Digital signatures captured for sign-off workflows
  • Photos uploaded by users for site documentation
  • User names and roles entered by administrators

How We Use Information

All data collected by this application is used solely for internal construction project management and daily field reporting purposes. Specifically:

  • To track daily site activity, manpower, and work performed
  • To manage punch lists and project task assignments
  • To generate and archive field reports for internal records
  • To back up submitted reports to an authorized Google Drive account

We do not use this data for advertising, marketing, or any commercial purpose outside of BCPS internal operations.

Data Storage

Data entered into the BCPS Field Log app is stored locally in the user's browser (via localStorage) and optionally backed up to a private Google Drive account controlled by BCPS. No data is transmitted to any third-party server other than Google when the backup feature is enabled by an administrator.

Google Drive integration uses OAuth 2.0 authentication. We do not store Google account credentials — only an access token provided by Google after authorization.

Google Drive Integration

When the Google Drive backup feature is enabled, submitted field reports are saved as JSON files to a designated BCPS Field Logs folder in the connected Google Drive account, organized by job name and date. This integration is:

  • Authorized only by an administrator via the Admin panel
  • Scoped to the drive.file permission — the app can only access files it creates and cannot read, delete, or modify any other Google Drive content
  • Governed by Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Who Has Access

The BCPS Field Log app is restricted to authorized BCPS employees and subcontractors. Access to the Admin panel is protected by a password set by BCPS administration. We do not make this application or its data available to the general public.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any data collected through this application with third parties, except as required by law or as necessary to operate authorized integrations (e.g., Google Drive) explicitly enabled by BCPS administration.
